Solmaris is an original open-world crime game built for the [make.fun gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) ("Make GTA 6"). A sun-soaked coastal city you explore on foot and by car: steal cars, outrun the police, run missions, and build your name across five districts.

Everything in it is original: the city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art, sound and music. No Rockstar Games name, logo, character, map or asset.

## Play

- On foot: WASD / arrow keys to move, E to enter a car.
- Driving: arcade handling, E to steal a parked or passing car.
- Wanted 0–5 stars: police chase, roadblocks, helicopter. Lay low to cool off.
- Missions: a six-mission story chain, "The Solmaris Job", with markers, objectives and payouts.
- Combat, shops, economy, HUD with circular minimap, save to localStorage.
- Sound is synthesized live with WebAudio — no audio files, no network.

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser, or run `node main.ts` to build the playable game and dossier into the output directory.
